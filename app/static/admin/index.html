Dedicated Management Hub

What Institutional Admins Manage Inside the Portal

Each approved administrator gets isolated, full-stack administrative controls tailored strictly to their own students and courses.

Visual Plugin Studio

Customize primary colors, brand hover states, position (bottom-right/bottom-left), and assistant name in real-time. Changes hot-reload onto student devices instantly without frontend builds.

Curriculum Knowledge & RAG

Upload PDF or TXT syllabus material. The backend automatically chunks and computes embeddings, ensuring student AI answers cite verified course readings and chapter numbers.

Live Tutor Escalation Desk

When students click "Live Help", your platform dashboard immediately receives the request. Tutors accept the session, view optional screen sharing, and message students back and forth in real-time.

Career Gateway & Accommodations

Post job opportunities, review student skill alignments, and process confidential interview accommodation requests for learners with disabilities or language preferences.

Telegram Student Linking

Enable mobile learning outside the browser. Students link their Telegram account with contact phone verification, allowing automated study prompts and out-of-band updates.

Strict Multi-Tenant Isolation

Strict database-level scoping ensures institutional administrators only see tickets, courses, and analytics for their assigned platform. SuperAdmins maintain governance across all tenants.
